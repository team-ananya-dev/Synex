# From Dev: customer-safe data, and what changed in the contracts

1. Customers must never see a name, a profile URL or an email. If they do, they can contact the expert directly and skip us. Today `GET /cases/{id}` and the event stream (`stage.done` results for `s3_sourcer`, `s4_people_screen`, `s5_ranker`, `s5b_enrich_rerank`) carry `name`, `headline` and `profile_url`. Treat those three fields as operator-only. Do not render them on any customer screen.
2. I am adding a customer-safe view next: an anonymised expert card per panel member (`person_id`, role label, company, tenure, `fit`, `score`, `evidence`, `reason`; no name, headline, profile URL or location beyond the country) and a redacted version of the event stream. I will document it in `contracts/API.md` as a new section. Build the customer screens against that when it lands, and use the full data only on the operator screens (approval queue, audit trail).
3. Rule 4 (competitor conflict) is in the people screen. `contracts/API.md` has an appended section with the new fields (`competitor_domains`, `current_roles`, the `rule_competitor` result and its `review` flag) and `contracts/EVENTS.md` has the new `competitor.lookup_failed` event. Everything is additive.
4. Mail: Mailpit will run locally (SMTP on 1025, web inbox on 8025) and nothing leaves the machine. The allowlist will hold two addresses, Dev's and yours. Outreach drafts will go through an approval queue (`GET /approvals`, `POST /approvals/{id}/approve` and `/reject`) that you will need on the operator screens. I will write the exact shapes into `contracts/API.md` before you build it.
